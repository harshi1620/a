Overflow Properties

Visible

This is a long paragraph that extends beyond the box. Since overflow: visible; is used, the extra content is displayed outside the box without being clipped.

Hidden

This is a long paragraph that extends beyond the box. Since overflow: hidden; is used, the extra content is clipped and cannot be seen.

Scroll

This is a long paragraph that extends beyond the box. Since overflow: scroll; is used, both horizontal and vertical scrollbars are always displayed, allowing you to scroll through the hidden content.

Auto

This is a long paragraph that extends beyond the box. Since overflow: auto; is used, scrollbars appear only when the content is larger than the box.
